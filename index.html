Voice Agent Bake-off

The same 30 line cook workers are screened by the current cascaded voice stack and by a Phonic-1 speech-to-speech screener. Every call is graded on 10 yes/no criteria, and a rollout gate decides whether the new screener may take the next step of traffic: replay, then 10% of live screens, then 50%.

Scorecard

What a screening team pays for: screens that finish, rules the screener follows, the approve or coach-call decision it makes, how fast it answers, and cost per finished screen.

Cost per finished screen = all minutes, including calls the worker abandoned, times the list price per minute, divided by finished screens. Current stack: $0.105/min, the middle of the Deepgram, OpenAI and ElevenLabs stack in Vapi's own pricing calculator, including its hosting fee ($0.082 to $0.129/min). Phonic-1: $0.065/min, Phonic's Pro list price. Latencies are illustrative.

Rollout gate

Prompt v1 was held back because one call promised a worker a shift. Prompt v2, replayed on the same calls, may move to 10% of live screens.

The 10 criteria

Written the way Phonic's Evals Guide asks: binary, observable, one behavior each. The same text is loaded into Phonic as eval prompts for the live run. "Not triggered" means the behavior never had a chance to occur (for example, nobody asked for another language).

Data

Synthetic calls. Faults were planted by hand so the scorecard has known answers to find. None of these numbers were measured on Vapi, Phonic, or any staffing marketplace. Prices are public list prices from phonic.ai/pricing and the pricing calculator at vapi.ai/pricing. Latencies, call outcomes and worker answers are invented.

Run it yourself

Python 3, no dependencies. The live scripts in live/ need a Phonic API key.

python3 -m unittest
python3 -m bakeoff.report
