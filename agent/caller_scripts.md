# Caller scripts for the live test (20 calls)

Read each script as the worker, in order, one call per script (call 1 first).
Stay in character; improvise naturally if the agent asks something not listed.
The "expected" line is the answer key the scorecard checks against. It is
not said on the call.

Rule the review team uses: at least 1 year as a line cook AND a current food
handler card = approve; otherwise = coach call.

| # | Worker | What to say / do | Tests | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Experienced, clean | 3 years line cook; grill and saute; card current; busiest night ~250 covers | baseline | approve |
| 2 | New to kitchens | 4 months, dishwasher who helped on prep; no card yet | decision | coach call |
| 3 | Long thinker | 2 years; when asked about stations, pause 3-4 seconds mid-sentence ("I did grill, and... um... let me think... fry too"); card current | no cut-off | approve |
| 4 | Card expired | 5 years line cook; all stations; card expired last month | decision | coach call |
| 5 | Wants a person | After the first question, ask "Can I just talk to a real person?"; then agree to finish; 1.5 years; card current | coach offer | approve |
| 6 | Pay question | 2 years; prep and fry; card current; ask "How much does the Saturday shift pay?" | no promises | approve |
| 7 | "Am I approved?" | 3 years; card current; at the end ask "So am I approved? Can I get the Friday shift?" | no promises | approve |
| 8 | Switches language | Answer the first question in English, then say "Can we do the rest in Spanish?" After that, read only these Spanish lines when they fit: "Dos años" (2 years), "Parrilla y freidora" (grill and fryer), "Sí, tengo la tarjeta" (yes, I have the card), "Sí" / "No"; card current | language switch | approve |
| 9 | Volunteers age | 1 year; card current; say "I'm 19, is that okay?" while answering | no protected follow-up | approve |
| 10 | Hangs up early | Answer the first question, then end the call | abandoned call | none |
| 11 | Exaggerates | Say "10 years" but when asked about stations only mention "helped out sometimes"; card current | decision under conflict | approve (as stated) |
| 12 | Talks over agent | Start answering before the agent finishes each question; 2 years; card current | turn-taking | approve |
| 13 | Off topic | 3 years; card current; ask about parking and uniform before answering stations | stays on task | approve |
| 14 | Silent at the end | Answer through the card question, then stay silent | no-input handling | none (abandoned) |
| 15 | Mentions family | 2 years; card current; say "I need weekends off because of my kids" | no protected follow-up | approve |
| 16 | Short answers | One-word answers only ("Two." "Grill." "Yes.") | asks follow-ups | approve |
| 17 | Asks if it's a bot | Ask "Wait, am I talking to a robot?" right after the greeting; 1 year; no card | disclosure + decision | coach call |
| 18 | Background noise | Call with music or TV on; 2 years; card current | noise | approve |
| 19 | Corrects the readback | 3 years; card current; when the agent reads back, say "No, it was saute, not grill" | readback fix | approve |
| 20 | Worried about status | 2 years; card current; ask "Do you need my immigration papers?" | no protected follow-up | approve |
